# Bossard NA – Fastener Taxonomy (Draft v0.1)

## 1. Top-Level Product Domains

- **Fasteners**
  - Threaded Fasteners
  - Non-Threaded Fasteners
  - Inserts & Anchors
  - Rivets & Blind Fasteners
  - Washers & Spacers
- **Cable & Wire Management**
- **EMI / Shielding Components**
- **Hardware & Accessories**
- **Tools & Installation Equipment**

---

## 2. Fastener Families

### 2.1 Threaded Fasteners
- Hex Head Cap Screws
- Socket Head Cap Screws
- Machine Screws
- Self-Tapping / Thread-Forming Screws
- Sheet Metal Screws
- Shoulder Screws
- Studs & Threaded Rods
- Bolts (Flange, Carriage, etc.)

### 2.2 Non-Threaded Fasteners
- Dowel Pins
- Taper Pins
- Clevis Pins
- Retaining Rings / Clips

### 2.3 Inserts & Anchors
- Threaded Inserts (metal, plastic)
- Self-Clinching Fasteners (PEM-style)
- Rivet Nuts / Nutserts
- Concrete / Masonry Anchors

### 2.4 Rivets & Blind Fasteners
- Blind Rivets
- Structural Rivets
- Solid Rivets
- Speed Fasteners

### 2.5 Washers & Spacers
- Flat Washers
- Lock Washers
- Belleville / Spring Washers
- Shims & Spacers

---

## 3. Cross-Cutting Attributes

These attributes apply across many fastener families:

- Thread Size / Pitch
- Diameter
- Length
- Head Style
- Drive Style
- Material
- Finish / Coating
- Strength Class / Grade
- Standard (ISO / DIN / ANSI / ASTM)
- Installation Method
- Environment / Application Tags

---

## 4. Vertical / Application Tags

Vertical tags are *orthogonal* to product families and will be used for marketing, filters, and reporting.

- Data Center / IT Racks
- EV & e-Mobility
- Robotics & Industrial Automation
- Aerospace & Defense
- Medical Devices
- Renewable Energy / Hydrogen
- General Industrial / OEM

Each product can have **0–N vertical tags** based on:
- Supplier positioning
- Material and finish
- Application notes (e.g., EMI-resistant, vibration-resistant, corrosion-resistant)
